3. AI: the game changer

From generative AI to agentic AI.
Generative AI responds: you prompt it, it produces text, code, or analysis, then it stops.

Agentic AI acts: given a goal, an agent plans the steps, uses tools, makes decisions, and executes a multi-step task on its own — with a human keeping oversight.
